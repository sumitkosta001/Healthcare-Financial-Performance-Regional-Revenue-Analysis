# Source Notes & Methodology: Dr. Lal PathLabs Financial & Operational Analysis (FY2023–24 vs FY2024–25)

## 1. Overview & Document Identification
This document accompanies the structured dataset extracted for the Healthcare Diagnostics Business Intelligence Dashboard. All figures are drawn directly from the audited consolidated financial statements and official management reports of **Dr. Lal PathLabs Limited**.

* **Report FY2023–24:** `reports/Annual-Report-2023-24.pdf` (336 pages)
* **Report FY2024–25:** `reports/Annual-Report-2024-25.pdf` (348 pages)

---

## 2. Unit Conversion Convention
* Source financial statements are denominated in **₹ million** (INR Million).
* Per instructions, figures are converted to **₹ crore** (INR Crore):
  $$\text{Value in ₹ Crore} = \frac{\text{Value in ₹ Million}}{10}$$
* Precision is retained from official disclosures without artificial rounding.

### Notes on Financial Metrics:
* **Statutory P&L vs MD&A Rounding:** The Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss reports exact decimals (e.g., FY24 PBT ₹5,054.51M / PAT ₹3,622.93M; FY25 PBT ₹6,247.99M / PAT ₹4,922.52M). The Management Discussion & Analysis (MD&A) tables present these rounded to the nearest integer million (FY24: PBT ₹5,055M, PAT ₹3,623M; FY25: PBT ₹6,248M, PAT ₹4,922M). Both representations match within 1 million due to standard rounding.
* **EBITDA:** EBITDA is a non-GAAP/operational measure reported in the Financial Highlights and MD&A sections as Reported EBITDA: ₹6,092M for FY24 (27.4% margin) and ₹6,956M for FY25 (28.3% margin).

## 5. Region-Wise Revenue Breakdown

Dr. Lal PathLabs discloses region-wise revenue under the Management Discussion and Analysis (MD&A) and Operational Highlights.

### Disclosure Formats Across Years:
1. **FY2023–24 Annual Report (Page 101 / PDF P. 101):** Disclosed exact absolute revenue in ₹ million along with percentage share:
   - Delhi NCR: ₹6,895M (31%)
   - Rest of North India: ₹7,005M (31%) *(Note: included UP & Uttarakhand)*
   - East India: ₹3,247M (15%)
   - West: ₹3,286M (15%)
   - South: ₹1,324M (6%)
   - Others: ₹508M (2%)
   - **Total:** ₹22,266M (100%)
2. **FY2024–25 Annual Report (Page 112 / PDF P. 112 & Page 23 / PDF P. 23):** Disclosed regional contribution in exact two-decimal percentages with Uttar Pradesh & Uttarakhand (UP & UK) reported as a separate regional category:
   - Delhi NCR: **31.08%** (FY25) vs **31.11%** (FY24)
   - UP & UK: **18.62%** (FY25) vs **18.52%** (FY24)
   - Rest of North India: **13.20%** (FY25) vs **12.87%** (FY24)
   - West: **14.62%** (FY25) vs **14.80%** (FY24)
   - East India: **14.55%** (FY25) vs **14.50%** (FY24)
   - South: **5.62%** (FY25) vs **5.94%** (FY24)
   - Others: **2.31%** (FY25) vs **2.26%** (FY24)
   - **Total:** **100.00%** (FY25) vs **100.00%** (FY24)

> [!NOTE]
> In accordance with strict data integrity guidelines, FY2024–25 absolute regional currency figures are not fabricated or multiplied out in the CSV because the official report directly provides percentage contributions. Both exact reported percentage contributions (FY24 & FY25) and reported FY24 absolute numbers are faithfully captured.
